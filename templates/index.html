Why Choose Us

MediCare is designed to modernize hospital outpatient departments (OPD). It removes paper queues, double bookings, and lost medical charts by establishing a single relational SQLite source of truth.

  • ✔ Zero double-booking slot validation
  • ✔ Complete patient medical history tracking
  • ✔ Itemized digital prescriptions and invoices
  • ✔ Simple role-based access control (Patient, Doctor, Admin)
